We tested two large language models (LLMs), GPT-4o-mini and SEA-LIONv3, on their handling of Indonesian-specific questions. Through human expert evaluation of 50 questions across four categories, we found that SEA-LIONv3 excelled at understanding local details and nuances, while both models performed comparably on general language tasks. This difference highlights the potential of localized LLMs like SEA-LIONv3 to enhance accuracy and cultural sensitivity in Southeast Asian applications.
