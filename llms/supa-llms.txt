# SUPA

> SUPA is an AI data infrastructure company that helps machine learning teams build high-quality training datasets through a blend of technology and expert human annotation. It provides data labeling and curation services — RLHF, LLM training, semantic segmentation, classification, and medical imaging — backed by domain-expert networks across healthcare, manufacturing, robotics, agriculture, design, and industrial sectors. SUPA does not publish a public developer API; engagement is through its sales/contact channels.
